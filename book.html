CHAPTER 2

The 2-Hour Work Week: The Master Workflow

The AI Content Machine is built on **Batching**. We do not create content daily. We create it once a month, in a single 2-hour "Deep Work" session. Here is your master workflow for producing 30 days of content in 120 minutes.

The 120-Minute Breakdown

  • Minutes 0–15: The Ideation Sprint (ChatGPT). Your first 15 minutes are spent finding 4 "Main Pillars"—the core topics you will talk about this month. Feed ChatGPT your niche and ask for Educational, Inspirational, and Controversial topics.
  • Minutes 15–45: The Writing Batch (ChatGPT). Now, turn those ideas into text. Use a "Mega Prompt" to tell ChatGPT to write the captions for all 12 ideas at once.
  • Minutes 45–90: The Visual Factory (Canva & CapCut). Open Canva and use the "Bulk Create" feature to turn your 12 ideas into stunning carousels in one go.
  • Minutes 90–120: The Automation Hookup (Notion & Make.com). Upload your assets to your Notion content calendar. Trigger your Make.com automation to queue all 30 days of content.
