## 📱 SECTION 1: SOCIAL MEDIA CAPTIONS (Prompts 1–10)

**Prompt 1 — Instagram Caption (Engagement)**
> Write 5 Instagram captions for a [fitness/finance/lifestyle] creator about the topic "[YOUR TOPIC]". Each caption should be under 150 words, include 1 question to drive comments, use a conversational tone, and end with a call to action. Include 10 relevant hashtags at the end.

**Prompt 2 — TikTok Hook Generator**
> Generate 10 powerful TikTok video hooks for the topic "[YOUR TOPIC]". Each hook must grab attention in the first 3 seconds, create curiosity or shock, and be under 15 words. Format: numbered list.

**Prompt 3 — Viral Twitter/X Thread**
> Write a 10-tweet Twitter thread about "[YOUR TOPIC]". Tweet 1: bold claim or surprising fact. Tweets 2–9: one key insight each (max 280 chars). Tweet 10: summary + CTA to follow or visit link. Make it punchy, shareable, and valuable.

**Prompt 4 — LinkedIn Professional Post**
> Write a LinkedIn post about "[YOUR TOPIC]" for [freelancers/entrepreneurs/marketers]. 150–200 words. Start with a personal story or bold statement. Share 3 lessons. End with a thoughtful question. Professional but warm tone.

**Prompt 5 — Pinterest Pin Description**
> Write 5 Pinterest pin descriptions for the topic "[YOUR TOPIC]". Each should be 100–150 words, include keywords for SEO, have a benefit-driven headline, and end with a URL placeholder. Tone: helpful and inspiring.

**Prompt 6 — Instagram Carousel Script**
> Write a 7-slide Instagram carousel about "[YOUR TOPIC]". Slide 1: attention-grabbing title. Slides 2–6: one tip or insight per slide (1 headline + 2 sentences each). Slide 7: summary + CTA to save or follow. Short, punchy, and visual.

**Prompt 7 — YouTube Community Post**
> Write 5 YouTube Community tab posts for a creator in the [NICHE] space. Mix: 2 questions to engage audience, 2 tips/insights, 1 poll idea. Each under 100 words.

**Prompt 8 — Facebook Group Value Post**
> Write a Facebook group post for [NICHE] creators that provides massive value without selling. Topic: "[YOUR TOPIC]". Include a numbered list of 5 tips. End with an open question. 200–250 words.

**Prompt 9 — Story/Reel Call to Action Script**
> Write 5 short Instagram Story scripts (10 seconds each) to promote "[PRODUCT/TOPIC]". Each must have: 1 hook line, 1 value line, 1 CTA. Format: Hook | Value | CTA.

**Prompt 10 — Monthly Caption Calendar**
> Create a 30-day Instagram caption calendar for a [NICHE] creator. For each day provide: Day number, Theme, Caption (75 words max), 5 hashtags. Organize in a table format. Tone: [CASUAL/PROFESSIONAL/INSPIRING].

## ✉️ SECTION 3: EMAIL MARKETING (Prompts 19–25)

**Prompt 19 — Welcome Email**
> Write a warm welcome email for new subscribers to a [NICHE] newsletter. Subject line options: give 5. Body: 200 words. Thank them, set expectations, deliver the free gift teaser, and share 1 quick win. Tone: like a friend, not a brand.

**Prompt 20 — Nurture Email Sequence (5 Emails)**
> Write a 5-email nurture sequence for [NICHE] subscribers leading to a $[PRICE] offer for "[PRODUCT NAME]". Email 1: Welcome. Email 2: Story/credibility. Email 3: Value/tip. Email 4: Case study/proof. Email 5: Soft pitch. Each email: subject line + 200-word body.

**Prompt 21 — Sales Email**
> Write a persuasive sales email for "[PRODUCT NAME]" priced at $[PRICE]. Include: attention-grabbing subject line (5 options), personal hook, 3 bullet benefits, urgency/scarcity element, and a strong CTA button text. 250 words max.

**Prompt 22 — Re-engagement Email**
> Write a re-engagement email for subscribers who haven't opened emails in 60 days. Subject: make it funny or bold (5 options). Body: 150 words. Acknowledge the silence, give them value, ask if they want to stay. Include unsubscribe nudge.

**Prompt 23 — Weekly Newsletter Template**
> Write a weekly email newsletter template for a [NICHE] creator. Sections: 1) This week's insight (100 words), 2) Recommended tool or resource, 3) Quote of the week, 4) What I'm working on, 5) Quick CTA. Tone: personal and conversational.

**Prompt 24 — Abandoned Cart Email**
> Write a 3-email abandoned cart sequence for someone who didn't complete purchase of "[PRODUCT]" at $[PRICE]. Email 1 (1hr later): gentle reminder. Email 2 (24hrs): address objection. Email 3 (48hrs): urgency + bonus offer. Include subject lines for all 3.

**Prompt 25 — Launch Announcement Email**
> Write a product launch announcement email for "[PRODUCT NAME]" launching on [DATE]. Build excitement, explain what it is, who it's for, the price, and include a big bold CTA. Add a P.S. with a bonus for fast action. 300 words.
