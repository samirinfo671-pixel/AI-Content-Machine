# CHAPTER 10: Package and Sell Your AI Skills

Having an "AI Content Machine" is a superpower. But you don't just have to use it for your own content. You can sell this expertise to others who are still struggling in the "Content Trap."

Here are the 5 most profitable income streams you can start today.

---

### 1. Sell AI Prompt Packs ($17–$47)
**The Concept:** People are lazy. Instead of learning how to write prompts, they would rather buy yours.
**How to start:** Collect your best 50 prompts into a PDF (like the bonus included with this book).
**Realistic Income:** $100–$500/mo (Purely passive).

### 2. AI Content Agency Services ($500–$2,000/mo)
**The Concept:** Small businesses need content but don't have the time. You run their "Machine" for them.
**How to start:** Reach out to local businesses (gyms, realtors, cafes). Offer to handle 30 days of content for a flat fee.
**Realistic Income:** $2k–$5k/mo (Requires 5–10 hours of work/week).

### 3. Sell a Digital Playbook ($27–$97)
**The Concept:** Package your exact workflow into an ebook or mini-course (exactly like this one).
**How to start:** Use your "1 Idea → 10 Outputs" system to promote your playbook on social media.
**Realistic Income:** Unlimited (Scales with your traffic).

### 4. Paid AI Workshops ($97–$297)
**The Concept:** People pay for speed. A 2-hour live workshop where you show them how to set up their own Machine is highly valuable.
**How to start:** Host a Zoom session once a month. Use your social media to drive sign-ups.
**Realistic Income:** $1,000–$3,000 per workshop.

### 5. "Content-as-a-Service" Newsletter ($9/mo)
**The Concept:** Send a weekly email with 5 viral hooks and 5 AI prompts specific to a niche.
**How to start:** Use Substack or Beehiiv to start a paid newsletter.
**Realistic Income:** $500–$2,000/mo (Recurring revenue).

---

### Pro Tip: The "Free to Paid" Bridge
Start by giving away a "Cheat Sheet" for free. This builds your email list. Then, once they trust your expertise, offer them the high-ticket services.
