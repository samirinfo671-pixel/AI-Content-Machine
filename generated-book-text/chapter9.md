# CHAPTER 9: Automated Email Welcome Sequences

Your social media is for **Attention**. Your email list is for **Income**. 

If someone joins your email list, they are a "Warm Lead." But if you don't talk to them for 3 days, they forget who you are. The solution? An **Automated Welcome Sequence** that sells for you 24/7.

### The 5-Email "Value & Sales" Sequence
Use these ChatGPT prompts to write your sequence in one go.

#### Email 1: The "Immediate Value" (The Welcome)
*   **Prompt:** "Write a short, friendly welcome email for someone who just downloaded my free 'AI Content Checklist'. Include the download link and one sentence about why this matters."
*   **Goal:** Build trust immediately.

#### Email 2: The "Origin Story" (The Why)
*   **Prompt:** "Write an email about my journey. I went from spending 40 hours a week on content to just 2 hours using AI. Explain the 'Aha!' moment when I realized automation was the key."
*   **Goal:** Create a human connection.

#### Email 3: The "Deep Dive" (The How)
*   **Prompt:** "Write a value-heavy email explaining the '1 Idea → 10 Outputs' system. Give them one actionable tip they can use today."
*   **Goal:** Establish your authority.

#### Email 4: The "Social Proof" (The Proof)
*   **Prompt:** "Write an email sharing a story of a client (or myself) who reclaimed their weekends by using the AI Content Machine. Focus on the result: more time with family/less stress."
*   **Goal:** Overcome skepticism.

#### Email 5: The "Soft Pitch" (The Offer)
*   **Prompt:** "Write an email that bridges the gap between the value I've shared and my full 'AI Content Machine Playbook'. Give them a 24-hour discount code."
*   **Goal:** Convert the lead into a customer.

---

### Implementation
Use a tool like **MailerLite** or **ConvertKit** (both have free tiers). Set the "Trigger" as "When a subscriber joins a group" and set the "Wait" time between emails to 24 hours.

Once this is set up, every person who joins your list gets the same high-quality experience, while you focus on creating more "Seed Ideas."
