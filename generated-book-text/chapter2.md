# CHAPTER 2: Your Daily AI Workflow Map

The secret to working 2 hours a week isn't working harder during those hours—it’s having a map that tells you exactly what to do and when to do it.

Without a map, you wander. With a map, you execute. Here is the exact daily breakdown of an AI Content Machine operator.

---

### The 75-Minute "Power Hour" Split
Most people think you need to sit at a desk for 8 hours to be productive. We’re going to do it in 75 minutes of total "human time," spread throughout your day (or batch it all on Monday).

#### 🌅 Morning: The Ideation Sprint (15 Mins)
**Goal:** Generate 10 high-quality content ideas and 1 master script.
*   **0-5 mins:** Open ChatGPT and feed it your "Ideation Prompt" (found in Part 2). Ask it for 10 hooks based on your niche.
*   **5-10 mins:** Pick the best hook. Ask ChatGPT to expand that hook into a "Master Script" (a 500-word deep dive).
*   **10-15 mins:** Copy the script into your Notion "Content Database."
*   **AI Leverage:** You’ve just done what used to take a human writer 2 hours in just 15 minutes.

#### ☀️ Midday: The Visual Batch (30 Mins)
**Goal:** Create 3 high-impact graphics or 1 carousel.
*   **0-10 mins:** Open Canva and select a template that matches your Master Script’s topic.
*   **10-25 mins:** Use "Magic Media" or your brand kit to customize the text. Don't overthink it; your Master Script has already done the heavy lifting for the content.
*   **25-30 mins:** Export the graphics as PNGs or MP4s.
*   **AI Leverage:** Canva’s AI-suggested layouts mean you don’t need to be a designer to look like one.

#### 🌇 Afternoon: The Automation Handoff (15 Mins)
**Goal:** Schedule the content across all platforms.
*   **0-5 mins:** Upload your graphics and captions into your scheduling tool (Buffer, Later, or your Make.com dashboard).
*   **5-15 mins:** Verify that your Make.com "Repurposing Scenario" is active. This scenario will automatically take your Instagram post and format it for Pinterest and LinkedIn.
*   **AI Leverage:** You are only "touching" the content once, but it is appearing in three different places.

#### 🌙 Evening: The Engagement Loop (15 Mins)
**Goal:** Reply to comments and build community.
*   **0-10 mins:** Use an AI browser extension or a simple ChatGPT prompt to draft thoughtful replies to the comments on your latest posts.
*   **10-15 mins:** Post those replies and engage with 5 other creators in your niche.
*   **AI Leverage:** AI handles the "heavy lifting" of drafting, while you provide the "human touch" of final approval.

---

### Why This Works
By breaking your day into these tiny sprints, you never feel overwhelmed. You aren't "making content" all day; you are simply checking the gauges on your machine.

If you choose to **Batch** this workflow, you can do all of this for the entire week on a Sunday afternoon in under 2 hours. That is the power of the machine.
