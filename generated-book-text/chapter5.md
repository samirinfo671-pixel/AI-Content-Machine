# CHAPTER 5: AI Video Script Formula

Short-form video is the "engine room" of the AI Content Machine. It is the only format that can reliably go viral and bring thousands of new eyes to your business overnight.

But most people fail because their scripts are too long, too boring, or have no clear call-to-action.

### The 3-Part Viral Formula
Every successful short-form video follows this structure:

1.  **The Hook (0–3 seconds):** You must stop the scroll. If you lose them here, the rest of the video doesn't exist.
2.  **The Value (3–50 seconds):** Deliver on the promise of the hook. Keep it fast-paced. One sentence per cut.
3.  **The CTA (50–60 seconds):** Tell them exactly what to do next. (Follow, Comment, Link in Bio).

---

### The Hook Generator
Use this prompt to generate 5 hooks for any topic:
> "I am making a Reel about [TOPIC]. Give me 5 viral hooks using these categories:
> 1. The 'Negative' Hook (e.g., Stop doing X)
> 2. The 'Secret' Hook (e.g., Nobody is talking about X)
> 3. The 'Result' Hook (e.g., How I got X in Y days)
> 4. The 'Relatable' Hook (e.g., POV: You're still doing X)
> 5. The 'Lazy' Hook (e.g., The easiest way to X)"

---

### Full Script Sample (Productivity Niche)
*   **Hook:** "I found an extra 5 hours in my work week and I’m never going back."
*   **Value:** 
    *   *Cut 1:* I stopped answering emails manually. 
    *   *Cut 2:* I used [Tool Name] to filter the junk and draft replies. 
    *   *Cut 3:* Then I set up an automation that moves my tasks into Notion automatically.
*   **CTA:** "Want the full tool list? Comment 'READY' and I'll DM it to you."

---

### How to Batch 10 Scripts in 1 Hour
1.  **Choose 10 Hooks** from your Hook Generator.
2.  **Feed them to ChatGPT** with this prompt: "Write 10 scripts of 60 seconds each using the 3-part viral formula for these hooks."
3.  **Record them all at once.** Don't change your outfit. Don't move your camera. Just read the scripts.
4.  **Edit in CapCut** using "Auto-Captions."

By doing this once a week, you have a daily video presence without the daily stress.
