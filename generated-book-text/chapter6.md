# CHAPTER 6: The Content Repurposing Machine

If you create a piece of content and only post it once, you are wasting 90% of your effort. 

**Repurposing** is the process of taking one "Big" asset and slicing it into "Small" assets for different platforms.

### The "Waterfall" Strategy
Imagine your content as a waterfall.
1.  **Top:** Long-form content (YouTube Video, Blog Post, Podcast).
2.  **Middle:** Mid-form content (LinkedIn Posts, Email Newsletters).
3.  **Bottom:** Short-form content (Reels, TikToks, Tweets, Pinterest Pins).

---

### Step-by-Step Workflow: 1 YouTube Video → 15 Assets

1.  **Transcribe the Video:** Use a tool like Descript or simply copy the transcript from YouTube.
2.  **The Slicing Prompt:** Feed the transcript into ChatGPT:
    > "Analyze this transcript. Extract 5 'Value Bombs' (key lessons). For each Value Bomb, create:
    > - 1 Twitter Thread (5 tweets)
    > - 1 Instagram Carousel Outline (6 slides)
    > - 1 LinkedIn Post
    > - 1 Pinterest Pin Title & Description"
3.  **The Visual Handoff:**
    *   Take the Carousel Outline and paste it into Canva's "Bulk Create" tool.
    *   Take the Twitter Thread and schedule it via Buffer.
    *   Take the LinkedIn Post and customize the intro for a professional audience.

### Why This Wins
You only had to be "creative" once (when you made the video). The Machine handles the distribution. This ensures that even if someone misses your YouTube video, they’ll see your LinkedIn post or your Instagram Carousel.

You aren't annoying your followers; you are reaching them where they are.
