# CHAPTER 4: Write a Month of Captions in 20 Minutes

The "blank page" is the enemy of consistency. In this chapter, we are going to use the **Caption Generator Mega-Prompt** to produce 30 days of high-quality captions in a single session.

### The Mega-Prompt
Copy and paste this into ChatGPT (Version 4.0 or Gemini 1.5 Pro recommended):

> "I want you to act as an expert Social Media Manager and Copywriter. I am a [INSERT YOUR NICHE, e.g., Fitness Coach] helping people [INSERT GOAL, e.g., lose weight without dieting]. 
> 
> Generate 30 unique social media captions based on the following 5 pillars:
> 1. Educational (How-to, Tips)
> 2. Inspiring (Story, Transformation)
> 3. Entertaining (Meme, Relatable struggle)
> 4. Promotional (Join my program, Buy my ebook)
> 5. Authority (Mistakes to avoid, Industry secrets)
> 
> For each caption: 
> - Provide a punchy HOOK (first line).
> - Provide the BODY text with line breaks.
> - Provide 3 relevant hashtags.
> - Ensure the tone is [INSERT TONE, e.g., Bold and Energetic].
> 
> Output this in a table format with columns: Day, Pillar, Hook, Caption."

---

### Customizing Your Tone
AI can sound "robotic" if you don't give it a personality. Here are three distinct "Tone Profiles" you can use:

*   **The Maverick (Finance/Business):** "No-nonsense, data-driven, slightly provocative, high-energy."
*   **The Mentor (Wellness/Lifestyle):** "Empathetic, calm, science-backed, supportive."
*   **The Hype-Man (Fitness/Entertainment):** "Direct, punchy, emoji-heavy, focus on action."

### Organizing in Notion
Once the AI generates the table:
1.  **Copy the Table.**
2.  **Paste into Notion.**
3.  **Drag and Drop** the rows into your "Content Calendar" view.
4.  **Batch the Visuals:** Now that you have the captions, you can go into Canva and create the matching graphics in one 30-minute block.

---
